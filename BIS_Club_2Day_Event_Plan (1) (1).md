# **1\. Overview & Objectives**

The BIS Club 2-Day Event is designed to build awareness among students about the role of standards (Bureau of Indian Standards) in everyday life, while encouraging creativity, teamwork, quick thinking, and public speaking through a mix of competitive and fun activities.

**Event Goals**

* Introduce students to the concept and importance of standards through hands-on and creative activities.
* Encourage teamwork, leadership, and healthy competition among participants.
* Provide a platform for creative expression (art, drama, rangoli) alongside academic rigor (quiz, debate).
* Recognize and reward outstanding performance across all events.

**Target Participants**

Open to all students, organized in teams for group events and individually for others.

## **4.2 Poster Making (Digital / Hand Drawing)**

| Day / Time | Day 1 — 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Auditorium (Hand Drawing) / The Forge Laboratory (Digital) |
| **Team Size** | Individual — choose either Digital OR Hand-drawn category |

**Objective**

To creatively communicate a standards/quality-related message through visual art, in either traditional or digital medium.

**Materials Required**

* Hand Drawing: Standard drawing kit (contents to be announced)
* Digital: Participants must bring their own laptops and any other necessities (e.g., drawing tablets, chargers)
* Reference topic chips/chits for topic draw
* Table, drinking water for participants

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Participants register at the venue and choose (or are randomly allotted) a topic from the list below.
2\. Hand-drawing participants collect an A3 sheet stamped/signed by the in-charge (to prevent pre-made entries).
3\. Digital participants show their starting blank canvas to the in-charge before beginning.
4\. 2 hours given to complete the poster; no reference to mobile phones/internet for content copying.
5\. Completed posters are labelled with participant name/team & topic, and submitted to the collection desk.
6\. Digital entries are exported as image files and submitted via a shared folder/pen drive/email.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* Why Standards Matter in Everyday Life
* ISI Mark — What It Means for Consumers
* Quality & Safety Go Hand in Hand
* Standards for a Sustainable Future
* Say No to Duplicate/Unsafe Products
* Hallmarking — Trust in Every Purchase

**Rules**

* No use of pre-made/traced artwork; must be original, done on the spot.
* Strict 2-hour time limit; late submissions are disqualified unless prior extension is granted by in-charge.
* Posters copied or taken directly from Google searches will be identified using Google Lens and disqualified.

**Judging Criteria**

* Relevance to theme (30%)
* Creativity & originality (30%)
* Visual appeal & neatness (25%)
* Message clarity (15%)

## **4.3 Debate**

| Day / Time | Day 1 — 1:30 – 3:00 PM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Seminar Hall |
| **Team Size** | Teams of 4 |

**Objective**

To develop critical thinking and public speaking skills while discussing real issues connected to standards, quality, and regulation.

**Materials Required**

* Mic & timer/stopwatch
* Score sheets for judges

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Teams of 4 members each must register in advance; topics are shared per organizer preference.
2\. Each of the 4 members per team is allotted 3 minutes to speak.
3\. Winning teams are selected based on the total team score.
4\. A "Best Speaker" award is provided to one individual based on performance and talent.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* Should mandatory quality certification apply to all consumer products?
* Voluntary standards vs mandatory standards — which drives better compliance?
* Is self-regulation by industry enough, or is third-party certification necessary?
* Should schools include 'standards & quality awareness' as a subject?

**Rules**

* Strict adherence to time limits.
* No personal remarks; arguments must stay on-topic.
* Fact-based arguments required.

**Judging Criteria**

* Content & argument strength (35%)
* Rebuttal & response to counterpoints (25%)
* Clarity, fluency & confidence (25%)
* Time management (15%)

## **4.4 Treasure Hunt**

| Day / Time | Day 1 — 3:15 – 5:00 PM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | All over college |
| **Team Size** | Teams of 6 |

**Objective**

To promote teamwork and problem-solving while reinforcing knowledge about standards through fun clues and tasks placed around the campus.

**Materials Required**

* 5 sealed clue envelopes placed at checkpoints around campus
* Clue sheet master copy with the answer key
* Small task props (e.g., a BIS-marked product to find, a puzzle piece, a QR code)
* Stopwatch, finish-line marker, walkie-talkies/phones for volunteers at checkpoints

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Each team is assigned a unique color; organizing team hides clues in advance at fixed checkpoints leading to the next point.
2\. Teams are briefed on rules and released; clues are different for all teams.
3\. Each team must complete 4 rounds of clues, where each clue is a riddle, question on standards, or simple task.
4\. Volunteer stationed at each checkpoint verifies the team has solved the clue correctly before handing over the next one.
5\. Final clue leads to the treasure marker at the finish point.
6\. The first team to reach the treasure wins.

**Rules**

* Teams must stay together at all times; splitting up leads to disqualification.
* No skipping checkpoints or checking other teams' clues.
* Out-of-bounds areas will be announced beforehand.
* Total time capped at 105 minutes; incomplete teams are ranked by checkpoints cleared.

**Judging Criteria**

* Fastest correct completion (primary criterion)
* Number of checkpoints cleared (tie-breaker)
* Adherence to rules / fair play

# **6\. Day 2 — Event Procedures**

## **6.1 Rangoli Competition**

| Day / Time | Day 2 — 7:00 – 9:00 AM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Auditorium side |
| **Team Size** | Teams of max 4 |

**Objective**

To showcase creativity and teamwork through traditional rangoli art, themed around standards/quality symbols (e.g., ISI mark motifs, Hallmark, colour-coded safety symbols).

**Materials Required**

* Rangoli powder/colours (multiple colours per team)
* Chalk for outline (optional)
* Measuring tape to mark out equal floor space per team
* Water, diyas/decorative items (optional, if allowed)
* Camera for judging documentation

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Each team is allotted an equal marked floor space in advance.
2\. Teams bring/collect their own rangoli materials.
3\. 2 hours given to complete the design from start to finish.
4\. Judges walk around at the end to evaluate all designs together.
5\. Photographs are taken of every entry for record and display.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* Free theme, OR themed: 'Standards in Nature', 'Safety Symbols', 'Unity through Quality'

**Rules**

* Only powder/petals/natural colours allowed — no paint on permanent flooring unless pre-approved.
* Team must stay within the allotted space.
* All 4 members should be seen contributing.

**Judging Criteria**

* Creativity & design (30%)
* Colour combination & neatness (25%)
* Relevance to theme (20%)
* Teamwork (15%)
* Time completion (10%)

## **6.2 Quiz**

| Day / Time | Day 2 — 9:00 – 10:30 AM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Forge Lab |
| **Team Size** | Individual |

**Objective**

To test participants' knowledge of BIS, standards, general science, and current affairs.

**Materials Required**

* Mobile phones for participants
* Projection screen for PPT
* Buzzinlive platform
* Quizizz or similar platform

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Conducted in two rounds.
2\. Round 1: Digital quiz for any number of students, conducted via mobile using platforms like Quizizz or similar.
3\. Round 2: Only for top selected individuals. Questions are displayed on a screen via PPT, and participants must use the Buzzinlive website to answer.
4\. Running scores are tallied to determine final individual rankings.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* BIS & the ISI mark
* Hallmarking of jewellery
* Indian and international standards bodies (ISO, IEC, etc.)
* General science & current affairs
* Consumer rights & product safety

**Rules**

* No conferring with other participants.
* Digital platforms (Quizizz & Buzzinlive) must be accessed only during the active round.
* Quizmaster's decision on ambiguous answers is final.

**Judging Criteria**

* Purely score-based — cumulative points across rounds determine ranking.

## **6.3 Learning Science via Standards**

| Day / Time | Day 2 — 10:45 AM – 12:30 PM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Auditorium |
| **Team Size** | Teams of 2–4 |

**Objective**

An event where participants build models based on specific topics and explain the underlying science behind them.

**Materials Required**

* Projector/screen for presentation
* Sample products for demonstration
* Test-kits or demo experiment materials
* Handouts/worksheets summarizing key concepts

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Short interactive talk on how standards are developed and tested scientifically.
2\. Live demonstration of 1–2 simple tests to show 'science in action'.
3\. Participants split into small groups for a short hands-on activity / model explanation.
4\. Quick recap / Q&A session to close.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* How products get tested before certification
* Basic quality control experiments
* Reading and understanding a standard mark/label
* Science behind everyday safety standards

**Rules**

* Participants must actively engage in the hands-on portion.

**Judging Criteria**

* Participation-based; optional short quiz/worksheet can be used to award a 'Best Learner' recognition.

## **6.4 Mimes**

| Day / Time | Day 2 — 1:30 – 3:00 PM |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Location** | Seminar Hall / Forge Lab |
| **Team Size** | Teams of 4–6 |

**Objective**

To creatively convey a message related to standards, quality, or safety through silent (non-verbal) performance.

**Materials Required**

* Stage/performance space
* Background music system (optional, no dialogue)
* Simple props as needed by teams
* Timer/stopwatch

**Procedure — How to Conduct**

1\. Each team registers a topic in advance or draws one on the spot.
2\. Teams get 3–5 minutes for their performance — strictly no dialogue or spoken words; only expressions, gestures, and optional background music.
3\. Performances happen back-to-back with brief set-up gaps between teams.
4\. Judges score immediately after each performance.

**Suggested Topics / Themes**

* Importance of quality checks before buying a product
* Consequences of using substandard/duplicate goods
* Journey of a product getting BIS certified
* Safety standards saving a life

**Rules**

* Absolutely no spoken words or sound effects that substitute for dialogue.
* Performance must stay within the 3–5 minute time limit.
* Content must be appropriate and non-offensive.

**Judging Criteria**

* Clarity of message conveyed without words (35%)
* Expression & body language (25%)
* Creativity & originality (25%)
* Team coordination & timing (15%)
