Day 1 · 9 September

Inauguration

9:00 – 10:00 AM
ParticipantsAll Participants
VenueMain Auditorium
Duration1 Hour
PrizeCeremonial

About the Event

To formally open the 2-day event, set the tone, and introduce the purpose of the BIS Club activities to all participants and guests. Includes lamp lighting, chief guest address, and a welcome ceremony.

Event Format

  1. MC welcomes the gathering and introduces the chief guest / dignitaries on stage.
  2. Traditional lamp lighting / inaugural ceremony by chief guest and club coordinators.
  3. Welcome address by Club President/Teacher-in-charge (3–5 min).
  4. Chief Guest Address (10–15 min) — talk on standards/quality in daily life.
  5. Brief overview of Day 1 & Day 2 events by the anchor.
  6. Vote of thanks by a student representative.
  7. Group photograph with all participants and guests.

Rules

  • All participants must be seated 10 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • Mobile phones on silent during the ceremony.
Day 1 · 9 September

Poster Making

10:30 AM – 12:30 PM
FormatIndividual
VenueAuditorium (Hand) / Forge Lab (Digital)
Duration2 Hours
Prize₹1,250

About the Event

Creatively communicate a standards/quality-related message through visual art, in either traditional or digital medium. Create an original poster on a BIS-related theme within 2 hours.

Event Format

  1. Participants register at the venue and choose (or are allotted) a topic from the list.
  2. Hand-drawing participants collect an A3 sheet signed by the in-charge.
  3. Digital participants show blank canvas to in-charge before beginning.
  4. 2 hours given to complete the poster; no mobile phones/internet for content copying.
  5. Completed posters are labelled with name & topic and submitted to the collection desk.

Suggested Themes

Why Standards Matter ISI Mark & Consumers Quality & Safety Standards for Sustainability No to Duplicate Products Hallmarking & Trust

Judging Criteria

30%Relevance to Theme
30%Creativity & Originality
25%Visual Appeal & Technique
15%Message Clarity

Rules

  • No pre-made/traced artwork; must be original, done on the spot.
  • No obscene or political content.
  • Only BIS/standards-related themes accepted.
  • Strict 2-hour time limit; late submissions are disqualified.
  • Posters copied or taken directly from Google searches will be identified using Google Lens and disqualified.

Day 1 · 9 September

Debate

1:30 – 3:00 PM
FormatTeam of 4
VenueSeminar Hall
Duration1.5 Hours
Prize₹1,250

About the Event

Develop critical thinking and public speaking skills while discussing real issues connected to standards, quality, and regulation. Teams argue for or against assigned topics in a structured debate format.

Event Format

  1. Teams register in advance; topics may be shared in advance or drawn on spot.
  2. For-team speaks first (3 min), followed by Against-team (3 min).
  3. 1-minute rebuttal each, then open floor/cross questions (if time allows).
  4. Timekeeper signals at 30 seconds remaining (single bell) and time-up (double bell).
  5. Judges score each speaker independently, then tally to declare the winning side.

Sample Topics

Mandatory quality certification for all products Voluntary vs Mandatory Standards Self-regulation vs Third-party certification Standards as a school subject

Judging Criteria

35%Content & Argument Strength
25%Rebuttal & Counterpoints
25%Clarity, Fluency & Confidence
15%Time Management

Rules

  • Strict adherence to time limits; judges may deduct marks for overtime.
  • No personal remarks; arguments must stay on-topic.
  • Logical, fact-based arguments — dramatics without content will be marked down.

Day 1 · 9 September

Treasure Hunt

3:15 – 5:00 PM
FormatTeam of 6
VenueAll over college
Time Cap105 Minutes
Prize₹1,250

About the Event

Promote teamwork and problem-solving while reinforcing knowledge about standards through fun clues and tasks placed around the campus. Race against time and other teams to find the hidden treasure!

Event Format

  1. Each team is assigned a unique color; clues hidden at fixed campus checkpoints.
  2. Teams briefed on rules and released; clues are unique for each team.
  3. Each team must complete 4 rounds of clues (riddles, questions, tasks).
  4. Volunteers at each checkpoint verify clue is solved before handing the next envelope.
  5. Final clue leads to the treasure marker at the finish point.
  6. First team to reach the treasure wins.

Judging Criteria

1stFastest Correct Completion
TieNumber of Checkpoints Cleared
+Adherence to Rules / Fair Play

Rules

  • Teams must stay together at all times; splitting up leads to disqualification.
  • No skipping checkpoints or checking other teams' clues.
  • Out-of-bounds areas will be announced beforehand.
  • Total time capped at 105 minutes; incomplete teams ranked by checkpoints cleared.

Rangoli

7:00 – 9:00 AM
FormatTeam of max 4
VenueAuditorium side
Duration2 Hours
Prize₹1,250

About the Event

Showcase creativity and teamwork through traditional rangoli art, optionally themed around standards/quality symbols (e.g., ISI mark motifs, Hallmark, colour-coded safety symbols).

Event Format

  1. Each team is allotted an equal marked floor space in advance.
  2. Teams bring/collect their own rangoli materials.
  3. 2 hours given to complete the design — no pre-drawn outlines allowed.
  4. Judges walk around at the end to evaluate all designs together.
  5. Photographs taken of every entry for record and display.

Judging Criteria

30%Creativity & Design
25%Colour Combination & Neatness
20%Relevance to Theme
15%Teamwork Visible
10%Completion within Time

Rules

  • Only powder/petals/natural colours — no paint on permanent flooring.
  • Team must stay within the allotted space.
  • All members should be seen contributing.

About the Event

Test participants' knowledge of BIS, standards, general science, and current affairs through a 2-round digital & interactive quiz format.

Day 2 · 10 September

Learning Science via Standards

Open Topic — Scientific & Engineering Principles via BIS Standards
10:45 AM – 12:30 PM
FormatTeam of 2–4
VenueMain Auditorium
Duration1.75 Hours
Prize₹1,250 (1st Place)

About the Event

Based on the official Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Learning Science via Standards initiative. Participants are free to choose any scientific or engineering principle behind everyday products aligned with BIS standards (e.g., Pressure Cookers IS 2347, LED Bulbs IS 16102, Electric Irons IS 302, Helmets IS 4151, Water Filters IS 14543, Solar Panels IS 14286, etc.). Build an educational prototype or display explaining how quality standards ensure performance, efficiency, and safety.

Choose Your Topic & Standard

Explore the official Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Learning Science via Standards repository to select your prototype topic, underlying science principles, and relevant Indian Standards.

Explore BIS Learning Science via Standards Topics

Event Format

  1. Teams set up their display at assigned stalls in the Auditorium before the event starts.
  2. Judges visit each stall; teams present a 5–7 minute interactive explanation of their prototype.
  3. Judges score based on scientific accuracy, prototype clarity, and connection to BIS standards.
  4. Results announced during the Valedictory function on Day 2.

Judging Criteria

30%Scientific & Engineering Accuracy
25%Prototype Clarity & Construction
25%BIS Standard Connection
20%Presentation & Q&A Defense

Rules & Safety

  • Use only low-voltage (safe) demonstration circuits — no live mains wiring without supervision.
  • All electrical/mechanical components must be safe for public viewing.
  • Content must be scientifically accurate and original.

Day 2 · 10 September

Mimes

1:30 – 3:00 PM
FormatTeam of 4–6
VenueSeminar Hall / Forge Lab
Performance3–5 Minutes
Prize₹1,250

About the Event

Creatively convey a message related to standards, quality, or safety through silent (non-verbal) performance. No spoken words — only expressions, gestures, and optional background music.

Event Format

  1. Each team registers a topic in advance or draws one on the spot.
  2. Teams get 3–5 minutes for their performance — strictly no dialogue.
  3. Optional: background music/sound effects allowed (no dialogue substitutes).
  4. Performances happen back-to-back with brief set-up gaps between teams.
  5. Judges score immediately after each performance.

Suggested Themes

Quality checks before buying Consequences of substandard goods Journey of BIS Certification Safety standards saving a life

Judging Criteria

35%Clarity of Message (without words)
25%Expression & Body Language
25%Creativity & Originality
15%Team Coordination & Timing

Rules

  • Absolutely no spoken words or sound effects substituting dialogue.
  • Performance must stay within the time limit.
  • Content must be appropriate and non-offensive.
